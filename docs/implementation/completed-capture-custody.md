# Completed physical capture custody

CPU2/3 physical dispatches now keep a collector/peer Busy completion in their original per-CPU capture cell. The cell distinguishes Active, Completed and Cancelled. Completed retains the first real times and dispatch binding; repeated finish cannot refresh them, consume warm twice or convert real execution into an unstarted cancellation. The exact storage remains included in `CAPTURE_CREDIT_BYTES` and the existing workspace bound.

Physical billing and evidence delivery are independent obligations. `begin_builtin` first attempts the already published Scheduler bill under the task gate, releases that gate, then retries capture delivery once. Capture Busy prevents a new grant or replacement while allowing the old bill to settle. A later owner turn checks active, pending completion and capture availability again under the admission gate. The original Scheduler sequence and physical CPU are charged once.

Warm is independently finished once. An owner token stays local only for Busy with the same pending, validated, non-invalid, retryable completion conditions used by the shared handoff. Peer success remains consumed when local collection is Busy. Terminal errors retain their existing missing/lost semantics. Collector/peer retries do not wait, call progress or execute under TaskGuard.

CPU2's disabled dispatcher must also retire its own capture before stop acknowledgement. After native stop readiness, `retire_cpu_capture(2)` independently attempts any old bill, releases TaskGuard and retries its local token. Busy prevents the stop tail. Existing task, IRQ and reference stop barriers remain authoritative. No other CPU reads the private capture cell.

The non-test host fixture executes actual OwnerAttempt, peer Attempt/Ledger, prewarm, dispatch gate, task gate, completion entry and admission bodies with a real Scheduler. A controlled `complete_locked` facade delegates billing to that Scheduler; it does not cover every production settlement-error branch. A full real 16-cell handoff and a busy collector reproduce the former Full loss. Tests retain the CPU3 completion locally, settle a TaskGate-delayed bill once, preserve warm once and original times, reject corrupted binding and a cancellation after completion, and resume new grants after independent owner recovery. A separate peer-Busy case preserves and releases its original lease. CPU2's actual disabled branch and stop-task prefix reject premature acknowledgement. GIC/TLB hardware is a controlled boundary in this fixture.

Negative controls remove completed-capture retirement protection, independent old-bill settlement, binding validation and stop-path retirement. Each fails its corresponding behavioral assertion. These host results do not prove native progress, fairness or a complete role transition.

CPU0 retains the original shared handoff path, so a collector suspended in I/O progress does not wait on its own private completion cell. The shared handoff remains 16 cells. This correction targets the observed physical CPU2/3 path; synchronous `measure`, IRQ and CPU0 completion capacity still require their own evidence and, if needed, separate bounded custody. No missing interval is accepted or hidden by this change.
